FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions
1. Who are these programs designed for?
Our programs cater to a wide audience including students, recent graduates, working professionals, government bodies, universities, and enterprises seeking to upskill or reskill their workforce.
2. What is the typical duration of a program?
Program lengths vary: from intensive 2‑5 day bootcamps and workshops, to multi‑week courses, and comprehensive job‑readiness programs that can span several months depending on the learning path.
3. Are the programs conducted online or in‑person?
We offer different programs with various flexible learning modes depending on each program type and it’s requirements including online, in‑person, and hybrid formats to suit different needs and geographies.
4. Do you provide certifications upon completion?
Yes. Most programs offer a certificate of completion. Key programs like Cultus Job Readiness (CJR) award a globally recognized 5‑Star Job Ready Badge valid for three years and trusted by 4,000+ employers.
5. What is the placement support like?
Eligible learners in job‑focused programs receive placement assistance, including profile sharing with our network of 4,000+ hiring partners, interview preparation, and career guidance.
6. Can organizations or institutions choose specific programs to create a custom curriculum?
Yes. We collaborate with governments, universities, and corporates to deliver tailored skilling solutions. You can select and combine programs from our catalog to build a custom learning pathway that meets your specific goals. Contact us to discuss your needs.
7. How do I choose the right program for me or my organization?
You can explore programs by category on this page, or reach out to our team for a personalized consultation based on your goals, background, and desired outcomes.
8. What makes Cultus programs different from other skilling platforms?
Our programs are built with direct input from industry (10,000+ recruiters consulted), focus on measurable outcomes (93% placement rate for eligible learners), and combine AI‑powered personalization with human mentoring.
9. How can I enroll or get more details?
Click the “Get in Touch” button on any program section, or visit our Contact Us page to connect with our team for guidance and enrollment support.
